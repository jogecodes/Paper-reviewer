This paper introduces \textsc{AutoCUSUM}, an automatic procedure for calibrating the reference value and decision limit of a CUSUM rule from a finite sample of normal scores, without anomaly labels or a user-specified target alarm rate. The resulting rule operates as a second-stage decision layer over the anomaly score stream. We evaluate the method across different scoring models and datasets, comparing it with alternative decision rules using observation-level detection metrics, false-alarm episode counts, and detection delay.
